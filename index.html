Adobe Express add-on

Score a design before you publish it

CampaignIQ reads the open Express document on your machine and returns a Campaign Readiness Score. Nothing is uploaded.

  1. Open a design in Adobe Express.
  2. Open the CampaignIQ panel from Add-ons.
  3. Pick Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, Poster, Slides, or Email.
  4. Click Run Analysis.
  5. Review scores, issues, and recommendations.
  6. Click Export PDF to download a local report.

What the score covers

  • Layout margins, alignment, crowding
  • Typography font count and hierarchy
  • Accessibility WCAG 2.2 contrast and alt text
  • Branding logo presence and consistency
  • CTA presence, size, placement
  • Platform fit word limits and aspect ratios
