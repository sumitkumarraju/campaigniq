Privacy policy

CampaignIQ is built to stay on the device. This page is the privacy policy for the Adobe Express add-on.

What we collect

The add-on does not create accounts, require login, or send document contents to a server. Analysis runs in the Express add-on iframe and document sandbox on your computer.

What stays local

  • The open Express document is read only to produce scores and recommendations.
  • PDF reports are generated in the browser and saved with a normal file download.
  • No advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs are included.
